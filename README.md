# Auroro Wallpapers

**Work in progress.**

The application UI is being rebuilt from scratch, one slice at a time, on top of the validated Kotlin data/domain layer (providers, discovery and validation engines, Room/DataStore persistence, settings, download/apply pipeline).

**Slices 1–10 are in:** dark-first Material 3 shell with a floating pill navigation bar whose selection indicator is a single sliding spring pill; Home feed with mode chips (For you / Trending / Newest / Random, each mapped to a real catalogue sort), an icon-only Any/Portrait/Landscape orientation filter in the header, and three Settings-driven layouts (stacked cards with hero pager, two-column grid, compact list). Cards show whole artwork (`ContentScale.Fit`) in containers that snap to each decoded image's REAL aspect, so bars and strips are gone even when CDN frames disagree with metadata; pull-to-refresh and double-tap-Home refresh the mix; previews are full originals decoded through a 2560 px cap for speed without softness; fullscreen detail with Favorite / Share / Download / Info circles, a big Set-wallpaper button, HDR window colour mode on HDR-capable displays, and honest whole-image preview; a crop editor with rule-of-thirds grid, 90° rotation (the applier maps the rotated rect back onto original pixels and rotates only the cropped region), and Home/Lock/Both icon cards; search with debounced `FeedPager` queries and a staggered grid; a cover-flow showcase with an icon-only All/Portrait/Landscape orientation filter; Library with Favorites, Downloads (All/Completed/Failed filter chips, live progress, retry/cancel/delete) and a real viewing History; and Settings that actually re-theme the app live — theme mode (light/dark/system), design presets, accent hue, AMOLED, contrast boost and font scale all route through a composition-local palette, and the Liquid Glass theme (slice 7): one backdrop engine — Haze 2.0.1 stable — behind a shared `auroroGlass`/`auroroHazeSource` abstraction, giving the pill bar, floating icon buttons and segmented controls real captured-backdrop glass (refraction tier, frosted tier, or opaque reduced tier per the now-functional Glass quality setting) with automatic fallback on unsupported devices. Slice 8 makes the glass the whole UI: Settings cards and the search field refract a slow-drifting aurora backdrop, the pill bar condenses to icons while the home feed scrolls down and re-expands with labels on scroll-up (the iOS Liquid Glass tab-bar behavior), tab transitions land in ~150 ms with a 60 ms exit so switching reads as instant, and the typeface setting is real — bundled Open Sans (Apache-2.0 variable font in `res/font`) or the system face. The catalogue grew from 2 to 7 rights-checked sources: Wallhaven, NASA APOD, Openverse (revived; CC0/PDM/BY/BY-SA gate), the Art Institute of Chicago (revived — deterministic IIIF URLs fix the missing-dimensions failure that retired it in 1.3.0), Wikimedia Commons (search-only, licence + minimum-size gated), The Met Open Access (`isPublicDomain` works only) and Cleveland Museum of Art (CC0). Per-source on/off lives in Settings as glass chips with per-catalogue marks (Material Design Icons vectors) over every integrated catalogue. Slice 9 made the bottom seamless: scrolling content fades into the background under the glass pill bar (no hard card edge line), every inactive chip and the header search button are real liquid glass, and tab switches are near-instant (150 ms in / 60 ms out). Slice 10 made every catalogue deliver: Openverse, Art Institute, Cleveland and The Met gained a seed-stable Random lane (so the Random tab and shuffle mix all seven catalogues), per-source page caches (6 h) plus a Met object-ID cache cut repeat API calls to zero, and `NewCataloguesLiveIntegrationTest` re-proves all five adapters against the LIVE public APIs on every CI run (auto-skipped offline). Wallhaven cards/carousels load the large thumb instead of the 4K original, APOD previews cap at 1440 px, Art Institute previews are server-resized IIIF at 1680 px, Wikimedia previews are 1600 px thumbs; detail/apply always use the original. Slice 11 made the shell feel instant: feed cards download each provider's sized thumb instead of multi-megabyte originals (the black-card fix, re-proved live in CI through the real image client), decoded card aspects are cached process-wide and applied without animation so recycled grid cards stay exactly where they were on scroll-back, refreshes and mode swaps keep the existing mix on screen until the new one lands (no empty-screen flash), Home and Search remember their scroll positions across tab switches, detail and cover-flow open on the preview rendition, and the system reduced-motion accessibility setting now drops slide transitions and pauses the infinite aurora drift. Slice 12 killed the repeats and the waiting: the random lane now draws a fresh seed per feed build and the page cache keys on that seed (so Random/shuffle can never replay a cached page, while sorted lanes still cache), explicit refresh rotates the For-you session, tab switches land in 110 ms with the pill indicator on a high-stiffness spring (selection reads instant at 60 or 120 Hz), the Home feed entrance rise plays only when the mix changes — never on tab return — every chip (mode, source, library filter, retry) is liquid glass in both states with an accent tint when selected, and Search no longer shows "No matches" for infrastructure honesty: a blank query shows recents plus tappable suggestions without touching the network, and rate-limited sources get a cooling-down glass card that retries itself when the wait ends. Motion follows Material 3 Expressive guidance (bouncy springs for small elements, emphasized easings for surfaces).
